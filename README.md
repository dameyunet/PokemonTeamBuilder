# Dameyune Taylor Individual Project
### Problem Statement
When playing the various pokemon games, first playthroughs involve exploring the game world and discovering new pokemon

while you build your team to take on the challenges given throughout the game. Often for experienced players

they already know going into their playthroughs what team they want to build and go looking for those specific pokemon 

throughout the game world. This often involves either knowing off the top of your head what, when, where, and how to

catch the hundred plus pokemon in each game or constantly checking back and forth various websites for information. This

projects goal is to provide a tool that can give players a way to quickly reference the pokemon they are trying to

capture in their playthroughs without needing to search multiple sites.
